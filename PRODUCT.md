# PRODUCT.md — HackNEX '26 Product Vision & Truth

## Executive Summary
**HackNEX '26** is India's premier national-level 3-day offline hackathon organized by NEXUS Club at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences (KITS), Coimbatore. Hosting 1,500+ student innovators, builders, and developers across India, competing for ₹1.5L+ prize pool.

## Target Audience
- Computer Science, AI, and Engineering students across India.
- High-impact hackers, UI/UX designers, and hardware/software builders.

## Core Brand Values
1. **Royal & Prestigious**: Feels like an elite invitation-only gathering of top talent.
2. **Cinematic & Modern**: High-contrast dark aesthetic, WebGL shader motion, and crisp typography.
3. **High Energy & Confidence**: Bold red/crimson accents `#ff1e42`, pure white highlights, and golden award highlights.

## Key Conversion Goals
- Team Registration (Teams of 4 members).
- Clear visibility of Schedule, Problem Statements, Sponsors, and Prize Pool details.
